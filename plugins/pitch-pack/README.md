# pitch-pack

A repeatable way to take a sparse client brief and turn it into a tight pitch package, spoken in the client's brand voice and shipped in the client's design system.

Born out of the work for New Murabba's Linear Park (Waha). Generalised so it works for any RFP response, agency pitch, or client-facing strategic communication project.

## What it produces

Three deliverables that work together:

1. **Creative Brief Hub** — the single source of truth the creative team reads first. A scrollable HTML page covering the project, the audience, the facilities, the phases, and the work the platform has to do.
2. **Comparable Launches** — a tight one-pager per reference project. Five to eight comparable launches studied for their communication style, positioning, and what is worth borrowing.
3. **Strategy Approach** — the strategy section of the pitch deck. Written as a scroll-page in the client's design system, then exported to an editable Keynote / PowerPoint.

All three sit inside the client's design system, not the agency's.

## The process underneath

Six stages, in order:

1. Read what is in front of you. Pull every fact from the brief, the RFP, the masterplan, and the supporting documents. Notice what is missing.
2. Name what is actually being solved. The brief states a problem. The strategic problem is almost always different.
3. Map the audience. Two to four concentric rings, each with a different job.
4. Study comparable launches. Five to eight reference projects, read for their communication style.
5. Write what the work has to do. Five to seven demands on the creative platform.
6. Pass it to the creative team. Three filters to read everything else through, plus a short list of things to avoid.

## Slash commands

- `/pitch` — start a new pitch project end to end
- `/pitch-strategy` — write or rewrite the Strategy Approach
- `/pitch-brief` — write or rewrite the Creative Brief Hub
- `/pitch-compare` — write or rewrite the Comparable Launches one-pagers
- `/pitch-deck` — export an HTML hub to an editable Keynote / PowerPoint
- `/pitch-clean` — run prose through the AI-tells filter before delivery
- `/pitch-port` — extract design tokens from a client brand brain
