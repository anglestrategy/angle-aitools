# Writing the Creative Brief

## What it is

The single document the creative team reads before doing anything else. A scrollable HTML hub that holds every fact about the project on one page, in the client's design system.

If the Strategy Approach is the synthesis, this is the source material the synthesis points back to.

## When to use

- Kicking off creative on a new pitch.
- Onboarding a freelance creative team mid-project.
- Sharing project context with a partner studio.

## The sections

Most creative brief hubs need roughly:

1. **Scoreboard.** What this pitch is being judged on. The criteria, the weighting if any, the format expected.
2. **The brief.** The actual brief, in a table. Eleven rows or so: campaign window, single-minded message, primary audience, reward, reason to believe, key insight, brand personality, what we do, what we don't do, deliverables, budget.
3. **Context.** A handful of small panels framing the moment — what is happening in the client's category, what is happening in the city or market, what is happening in the brand.
4. **Anatomy.** What the project actually looks like, ideally with a diagram. The physical or product anatomy in plain language.
5. **Facilities or features.** What is on the ground (or in the product) at launch. A categorised list, not marketing copy.
6. **Comparable launches.** A short link out to the longer Comparable Launches deck if there is one. Two or three sentences here is enough.
7. **Phases.** The rollout calendar. What happens at launch, what happens six months in, what happens at year one. Helps the creative think across time.
8. **The cornerstone.** The single strategic note the work has to honour. One headline plus two short columns.

Skip any section the project does not need. Add sections the project demands.

## Voice

Plain, factual, no salesmanship. The creative team reads this for facts, not for inspiration.

**Required pass before delivery:** run every word through `cutting-the-AI-out-of-the-prose`.

## Template

`template.html` has the scaffolding for all eight sections plus the sticky nav and the cover. Replace the design tokens with the client's (see `porting-a-client-design-system`) and fill in the content.
