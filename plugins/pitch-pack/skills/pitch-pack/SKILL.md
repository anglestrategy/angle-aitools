# pitch-pack

A repeatable way to turn a sparse client brief into a tight pitch package, spoken in the client's brand voice and shipped in the client's design system.

## When to use

Use when the work involves any of:

- A client RFP that needs a full response, not just a deck.
- An agency pitch where the strategy and the creative need to be packaged together.
- A launch brief (real estate, destination, product, public realm, cultural project) that needs a creative platform that scales beyond the launch moment.
- Any project where the deliverable is a multi-asset pitch hub rather than a single document.

Not for: a single email, a single deck slide, a tactical campaign, or internal-only strategy work.

## The six stages

1. **Read what is in front of you.** Pull every fact from the brief, RFP, masterplan, supporting documents. Notice what is missing. Build a list of questions for the client without breaking the flow.
2. **Name what is actually being solved.** The brief states a problem. The strategic problem is almost always different. Write the real problem as a single sentence.
3. **Map the audience.** Almost always two to four concentric rings. Each ring has a different reason to be there and a different thing it needs to walk away with.
4. **Study comparable launches.** Pick five to eight reference projects from the same category. For each, write *how they positioned it* and *what we take from it*. Tight reads, not detailed case studies.
5. **Write what the work has to do.** Five to seven demands on the creative platform. These are the briefs underneath the brief.
6. **Pass it to the creative team.** Three filters to read everything else through, plus a short list of things to avoid.

## The three deliverables

- **Creative Brief Hub** — see `writing-the-creative-brief`
- **Comparable Launches** — see `studying-comparable-launches`
- **Strategy Approach** — see `writing-the-strategy-approach`

All three ship in the client's design system. See `porting-a-client-design-system`.

If the client wants an editable Keynote, run the Strategy Approach HTML through `exporting-html-hubs-to-keynote`.

## Non-negotiable: voice

Every word that leaves the studio runs through `cutting-the-AI-out-of-the-prose` first. No em dashes, no clipped "not X, Y" pairs, no triadic rhythm, no pitch-deck abstractions. Plain, direct, confident.

## Working order

Start with the Creative Brief Hub (gets the facts on one page).
Then Comparable Launches (calibrates the bar).
Then Strategy Approach (the synthesis).
Then export to Keynote if needed.

For a worked example, see the Waha / Linear Park / New Murabba project — every output in the suite is in the project folder.
